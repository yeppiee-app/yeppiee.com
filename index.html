How we use your info

  • For each child we only ask for a first name or nickname, an age and interests. No last names, photos, schools or exact addresses.
  • We use your email to send the options you asked for and to answer your questions. No newsletters unless you ask for them.
  • We never sell your information. Your request is stored privately and only seen by the Yeppiee team.
  • We use AI to research programs. It sees your children's nicknames, ages and interests and your general area, never your email address.
  • Want your request deleted? Email info@yeppiee.com and we'll remove it.
